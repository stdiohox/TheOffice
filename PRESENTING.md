# Presenting the demo

Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Open it once before the meeting: the first load fetches about 11 MB
and after that it opens at once. Do not use a private window, because the demo saves in the browser.

All sample logins use the password `demo1234`, and the sign-in page has one-press buttons for the main people.

## The controls

The **Demo** pill at the top of the screen opens the presenter's controls. **Shift+D** hides them.

- **Guided tour**: 12 stops. It changes person and page for you and tells you what to say.
- **Be someone**: Ngozi (CEO), Bisi (HR), Tunde (ops), Amaka (team member).
- **Open a second window**: put two people side by side and knock from one to the other. This is the
  moment that sells it.
- **Move the clock**: the office locks reports to set hours, so never mind what time your meeting is.
  The team files their reports for the time you skip.
- **Make something happen**: someone knocks, or a task comes in.
- **Reset the demo**: back to the start, in about 10 seconds. Do it before each meeting.

## A ten-minute run

1. Reset. Sign in as **Ngozi**. Show the floor: who is in, what is on each door.
2. Open a second window as **Amaka**. She is locked out until she files a sign-in. File it.
3. Amaka knocks on Ngozi. Let her in. Chat.
4. As Ngozi, give Zainab a task. Keep talking: within half a minute she has started and finished it. Approve it and give it stars.
5. Ngozi posts a notice that must be read. Watch the count climb.
6. Be **Bisi**. Show Reports, then Insights, then approve a time off request.
7. In **Rooms**, try the Focus room as Ngozi: even the CEO is asked for a code. Enter it from the Demo controls (Room codes) and read back the conversation. Say something; Zainab or Dayo answers. Invite Amaka: her window gets a pop-up, Join puts her in the room with no code, and the invite email is in the Email log.
8. Move the clock to **19:10**, then open **Email log**: the summary, the alerts, each one as it would arrive.
9. **Fri 19:10**: the leaderboard closes and everyone gets their week.

## What to say if asked

- *"Is this real data?"* No. A fictional company and team. The software is real.
- *"Does it really email people?"* In the real office, yes. Here each email stops at the log so you can read it.
- *"Are the new pages in production?"* Insights, Notices, Time off, Rooms and Team chat are built here,
  with real database rules, and are not yet in a client's live office. Say so.
- *"Is it watching my staff?"* It records reports, presence inside the office and declared status. Never
  screens, keystrokes, webcams or other apps. Open the staff notice to show it.
- *Who gets into a room?* The first person in opens it and gets a new six-digit code. Anyone else needs that code or an invite. Five wrong tries locks them out of that room for 10 minutes.
- *Can managers read room conversations?* No. Only people inside a room can read it, and it is deleted when the last person leaves. Someone who steps in can read back, so people should know that.
- *Video in Rooms?* Rooms are text conversations, with bookings and invites. There is no video in the demo; a rollout links your meeting tool.
